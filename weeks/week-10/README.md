# Week 10: Final Project Presentations

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Effectively communicate complex epigenetic concepts to diverse audiences
- Synthesize knowledge from across the course through independent projects
- Critically evaluate scientific communication approaches and tools
- Present and discuss final project work with peers

## Key Topics
1. **Final Project Presentations**
   - Student presentations of completed projects
   - Peer feedback and discussion
   - Reflection on learning and applications

2. **Science Communication and Public Engagement**
   - Strategies for communicating complex science
   - Adapting content for different audiences
   - Balancing accuracy and accessibility

3. **AI Tools in Scientific Research**
   - Applications of AI in literature synthesis
   - Critical evaluation of AI-generated content
   - Future role of AI in research workflows

4. **Course Synthesis and Reflection**
   - Integration of concepts from across the course
   - Future directions in marine epigenetics
   - Applications to conservation and management

## Required Readings
- Selected readings on science communication and public engagement
- Resources on AI tools in academic research
- Course materials from previous weeks as needed for project development

## Final Assignment

Students will complete **one** of the following project options and present their work during Week 10:

### Option 1: Public Communication / SciComm Project

**Goal**: Communicate complex epigenetic-related science to broader audiences.

**Assignment Requirements**:
Create a public-facing deliverable (choose one format):
- **Blog post**: 800-1200 words for a general audience (e.g., Medium, personal blog)
- **Infographic or short video**: 2-3 minute visual communication piece
- **Podcast episode**: 5-10 minute audio segment explaining a key concept

**Reflection Component**:
Include a written reflection (500-750 words) addressing:
- Target audience selection and rationale
- Strategies for framing complex concepts accessibly
- How you maintained scientific accuracy while simplifying
- Challenges encountered and lessons learned

**Deliverables**:
- Final public communication piece
- Written reflection
- Brief (5-7 minute) presentation to class

### Option 2: AI-Integrated Scholarly Reflection

**Goal**: Meta-analysis and digital scholarship using AI tools.

**Assignment Requirements**:
Use AI tools (ChatGPT, NotebookLM, Claude, or similar) to:
1. **Summarize literature** on a seminar theme from the course (minimum 5-7 papers)
2. **Critically evaluate** the AI tool's output for:
   - Accuracy of scientific content
   - Completeness of coverage
   - Potential biases or misrepresentations
   - Quality of synthesis

**Reflection Component**:
Write a 1000-1500 word reflection addressing:
- Which AI tool(s) you used and why
- The prompting strategy and iteration process
- Specific examples of accurate and inaccurate outputs
- Comparison with your own understanding of the literature
- How AI might reshape research synthesis in marine epigenetics
- Ethical considerations and limitations

**Deliverables**:
- AI-generated literature summary (with prompts documented)
- Critical evaluation and reflection paper
- Brief (5-7 minute) presentation to class

## Presentation Format

Each student will present their final project (5-7 minutes):
- Overview of project approach and goals
- Key findings or outcomes
- Challenges and insights gained
- Questions for class discussion

Followed by:
- Peer questions and discussion (3-5 minutes)
- Constructive feedback from classmates

## Discussion Topics

1. What makes science communication effective for different audiences?
2. How can we balance scientific accuracy with accessibility in public communication?
3. What role should AI tools play in literature synthesis and research?
4. What are the ethical considerations when using AI in academic work?
5. How has your understanding of marine epigenetics evolved throughout the course?
6. What future research directions seem most promising or important?

## Assessment

### Evaluation Criteria
**For Option 1 (SciComm Project)**:
- Clarity and accessibility of communication
- Scientific accuracy maintained
- Appropriate for target audience
- Creativity and engagement
- Quality of reflection on process

**For Option 2 (AI-Integrated Reflection)**:
- Thoroughness of literature coverage
- Critical analysis of AI outputs
- Depth of reflection on AI's role
- Documentation of process and prompts
- Insights on future implications

### Peer Evaluation
- Constructive feedback on classmate presentations (formative)
- Participation in discussion and questions

### Course Reflection Essay 
Brief essay (500-750 words) on:
- Key learnings from the course
- How course content might apply to your research
- Suggestions for future course improvements
